# Privacy Policy for CattitudeFlow

## Last Updated: 2026-05-11

## 1. Data Collection and Usage
CattitudeFlow is designed with privacy as a core principle. This application \*\*does not collect, store, or transmit\*\* any personal information, notes, or metadata to external servers or third parties.

## 2. Local Storage
 All notes and information you create within the application are stored \*\*locally on your device\*\*. You have absolute ownership and control over your data.

## 3. Third-party Services
The application does not use any third-party analytics, advertising networks, or tracking tools. 

## 4. WebView2 Usage
CattitudeFlow uses Microsoft Edge WebView2 for its user interface. This component operates within the standard security boundaries of your operating system.

## 5. Contact
 For any questions regarding this policy or the application, please visit our \[GitHub Repository Issues](https://github.com/nekomaru555555-web/CattitudeFlow/issues).

## 6. Changes to This Policy
We may update this policy from time to time. The core principle of "no data collection" will remain unchanged.
